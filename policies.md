# Policies

ICER follows the policies of ACM and the ACM Special Interest Group on Computer Science Education (SIGCSE). The documents are maintained by those organizations; this page links to them.

## Conduct and ethics

- [ACM Policy Against Harassment](https://www.acm.org/about-acm/policy-against-harassment)
- [ACM Code of Ethics](https://www.acm.org/code-of-ethics)

## Conference policies

These SIGCSE policies apply to all SIGCSE conferences, including ICER. The full list of SIGCSE policies is on the [SIGCSE Policies and Guidelines](https://sigcse.org/policies/) page.

- [Conflict of Interest](https://sigcse.org/policies/COI.html)
- [Program Chair Responsibilities](https://sigcse.org/policies/pcr.html)
- [Presenter Substitution](https://sigcse.org/policies/substitute.html): presenter substitution and no-show policy
- [Committee Remuneration Guidelines](https://sigcse.org/policies/remuneration.html)
- [Corporate Sponsorship](https://sigcse.org/policies/corporate.html)
- [Volunteer Travel Policy](https://sigcse.org/policies/travel.html)
