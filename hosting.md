# Hosting ICER

We are always eager to consider proposals to host ICER, whether you have organized a conference before or would be doing so for the first time. ICER rotates between North America, Europe and Australasia, and each edition is shaped by the community that hosts it.

The Steering Committee is happy to consult on any aspect of a site bid, from choosing a venue and dates to building a budget and assembling a local team. Informal conversations before you submit a proposal are welcome; see [Contact](contact).

## Proposal process

1. Contact the Steering Committee chair to express interest.
2. Submit a proposal covering the items below.
3. The committee reviews proposals and forwards a recommendation to the SIGCSE Board.

## What to include

- Proposed dates and venue
- Local organizing team
- Preliminary budget
- Travel, accessibility and visa considerations
- Support for hybrid participation

## Guidance

- [Conference Chair Selection](https://sigcse.org/policies/chairs.html) (SIGCSE)
- [Policy on Replacement of Program and Site Chairs](https://sigcse.org/policies/icer-chair-replacement.html) (SIGCSE)
- [Establishing an ACM event](https://www.acm.org/special-interest-groups/volunteer-resources/conference-planning/section-1-establishing-an-acm-event) (ACM conference planning guide)

## Timeline

We put out a call for site proposals each year, typically in July, the month before ICER. We aim to settle on a site three years before the conference is held there.
