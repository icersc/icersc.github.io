# Hosting ICER

We are always eager to consider proposals to host ICER, whether you have organized a conference before or would be doing so for the first time. ICER rotates between North America, Europe and Australasia, and each edition is shaped by the community that hosts it.

The Steering Committee is happy to consult on any aspect of a site bid, from choosing a venue and dates to building a budget and assembling a local team. Informal conversations before you submit a proposal are welcome; see [Contact](contact).

## Timeline

We put out a call for site proposals each year, typically in July, the month before ICER. We aim to settle on a site three years before the conference is held there. The committee reviews proposals and forwards a recommendation to the SIGCSE Board.

## What to include

Each call invites proposals to host ICER (site proposals) and proposals to serve as program committee (PC) chair. We welcome standalone site or PC chair proposals, and we also encourage combined proposals, where a group of researchers jointly proposes to host the conference and organize its academic program. Members of such a group need not be affiliated with the same institution.

Submit proposals by email to [icer-sc@listserv.acm.org](mailto:icer-sc@listserv.acm.org). Each call gives the deadline for that cycle.

### Site proposals

1. **General Chair:** name, affiliation and CV.
2. **Location:** city, state or province, and country.
3. **Venue:** the expected type of host venue (for example, a university, resort or cruise ship) and the approximate cost of venue rental and audio-visual services.
4. **Travel accessibility:** how easily participants can reach the location, including proximity to likely airports and train stations for attendees.
5. **Lodging:** an overview of nearby hotels and other lodging, with estimated daily costs for low-, medium- and high-end accommodation.
6. **Format flexibility:** the Steering Committee may ask sites to support different forms of participation, including synchronous or asynchronous hybrid participation, as well as new session types or conference structures, such as days when the conference runs in a multi-track format. Describe what your site and host team can do to accommodate these possibilities.

### Program committee chair proposals

Include a CV and a cover letter. The cover letter must address your qualifications and may set out your vision for innovation in the content or structure of the ICER program. Desirable qualifications include:

1. Prior attendance at ICER
2. Prior publication at ICER
3. Past service on the ICER program committee and other program committees
4. Evidence of research excellence in computing education
5. Collaborative and organizational skills sufficient to work on the organizing committee and to share oversight of the program selection process
